# Model card: lead conversion v6

Generated by `python -m ml card` from the training record of 17 Nov 2026. Registry: MLflow model `lead_conversion`, alias `champion`.

## Intended use

Ranks each salesperson's open inquiries every morning by expected fee (chance to book × fee), with three plain reasons and a suggested next action (Spec 04 §7). Users: the sales team (2 salespeople, about 15 follow-ups a day each) and the sales manager.

**Not for:** deciding whom *not* to serve. Every couple stays reachable: low-scored leads remain on the list, and 10 % of inquiries are a random holdout worked newest-first (the A/B comparison, Spec 04 §8).

## Data and label

- Unit: an open inquiry on a score date (features as of 00:00, point in time). Label: a booking by the couple within 60 days (window chosen by the pre-registered EDA rule, `docs/v2/ml_eda.md`).
- Training window 2022-07-02 to 2026-01-20 (50,172 rows, sampled ages); test window 2026-03-22 to 2026-09-17 (23,293 daily rows, out of time, 60-day purge, no inquiry on both sides).
- Algorithm: lightgbm (chosen on 5-fold rolling-origin CV: LightGBM 0.409 ± 0.062, logistic regression 0.394 ± 0.076); isotonic calibration.

## Monitoring and retraining

Daily: PSI per feature against the test-period population, missing shares, values outside the training range, score mix. Weekly: performance on matured labels, overall and on the holdout. Weekly retraining (Sunday) through the same gate; the previous champion keeps the alias `previous` (`python -m ml rollback`). Results: `ml.monitor`, Data & ML page.

## Limits

- Simulated data: the drivers are assumptions (Spec 06); the method, not the coefficients, carries over to a real CRM.
- The CRM keeps only the latest quote and budget; edits after the cutoff would leak (none in the simulation; a Quote table fixes it).
- Calibration plateaus (isotonic) give ties; the list breaks them by expected fee.
- Feedback loop: the list changes who is called; the holdout keeps unbiased data for monitoring.

## Personal data

Features hold no names, phone numbers or text. Message text is read only by the masked restricted Silver table for the intent tagger and experiments; couple names reach only the Sales role, at display time.
